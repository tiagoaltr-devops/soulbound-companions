Adaptation for the Lupus Pleno visual in Soulbound Companions.
The adapted Brambleback model geometry and model animation files
(BramblebackPlenoGeometry.java, BramblebackPlenoModel.java), the associated importer
(tools/import-brambleback-pleno.ps1) and the redistributed texture
(textures/entity/lupus_pleno_brambleback.png) are provided under CC BY-SA 4.0.
These asset-specific terms are an exception to the project's general license.

Changes: native Java geometry conversion, coordinate/pivot/rotation conversion,
exclusion of the editor's auxiliary hitbox mesh, original texture extracted
unchanged, procedural animations replacing the editor's run/attack clips.
No gameplay mechanics or functional collision dimensions were imported.
The source describes this as Brambleback from League of Legends. The listing's
license is attributed to its uploader; no endorsement is implied.
